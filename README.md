One complete story in exactly six words, built for the [make.fun "Six Words, One Story" bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-deranalabs-six-words-one-story-f982e410). A man buries a gun — and it blooms.

## The story

> He buried the gun. It bloomed.

Six words, 30 characters, one arc: a past act of violence, hidden away — then life grows out of what he tried to bury. The twist lands on the last word: "bloomed" turns the burial into growth, and leaves the reader to fill in whose gun it was, what it did, and what exactly bloomed.

## Run

`main.ts` (Node 22, no network, no dependencies) writes the six-word story to `/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
